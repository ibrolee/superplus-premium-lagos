# Super Plus Fitness Website Rebuild

## Goal

Build a complete, production-quality multi-page website for Super Plus Fitness & Spa that feels distinctly Lagos, premium, physical, and welcoming while preserving every existing public and member-facing concept without pretending unavailable backend systems work.

## Visual Direction

- Use a unified charcoal, white, and strong warm-red accent system with bold condensed editorial headlines and clean sans-serif body text.
- Pair cinematic, tightly cropped fitness and recovery photography with restrained graphic details, generous whitespace, square-to-subtle-radius cards, and minimal effects.
- Create intentionally different desktop, tablet, and mobile compositions, including compact navigation, prominent join actions, and easy contact access.
- Add only subtle reveals, image movement, and button/card transitions, with reduced-motion support.

## Shared Foundation

- Build reusable public navigation, mobile menu, footer, calls-to-action, section headings, membership cards/grids, service cards, facility cards, testimonials, gallery, forms, modal, location, and notifications.
- Create centralized typed data for contact details, hours, membership plans, spa/recovery services, facilities, and testimonials.
- Store all supplied membership prices and benefits once. Mark Quarterly as “Best Value.”
- Keep spa prices in the same central source once the user supplies them; do not invent amounts.
- Create locally stored, cohesive image assets for fitness, training, spa, recovery, facilities, members, and brand storytelling.

## Public Pages

- **Home:** Build all eleven requested sections in order, with category-based membership preview, editorial facility grid, personal training split, recovery services, testimonials, HMO conversion band, location actions, and final CTA.
- **Membership:** Add category tabs for All, Gym, VIP, Family, Training, and Spa & Recovery; provide clear duration, benefits, registration fee, and non-payment registration CTAs.
- **Personal Training:** Build the requested training story, process, inclusions, experience, and conversion sections.
- **Facilities:** Build an image-led masonry/editorial gallery with replaceable centralized media.
- **Spa & Recovery:** Give every listed service an image, description, centrally managed price, and enquiry CTA.
- **About:** Build a photography-led brand story covering the five requested topics.
- **HMO:** Build a professional decision-maker page with partnership process, facility proof, available services, and an enquiry form.
- **Contact:** Add business information, hours, map embed, WhatsApp/call/directions actions, and a validated contact form.

## Member and Reception Surfaces

- Add `/login`, `/member`, `/my-qr`, and `/reception-checkin` as visually consistent integration-ready pages.
- Clearly label unavailable authentication, purchasing, QR, and check-in actions as connection points rather than working systems.
- Make the member QR/status summary the first dashboard content and support a full-screen mobile QR display.
- Create reception scanner, active, expired, and invalid presentation states without fake scanning behavior.

## Routing, Conversion, and Forms

- Use real internal links for every navigation item and CTA; avoid broken or hash-only primary navigation.
- Route join and plan actions into a clear registration handoff placeholder, never a fake checkout.
- Use call, email, WhatsApp, and Google Maps direction links for real contact actions.
- Validate enquiry/contact forms in the interface and explain that submission delivery awaits backend connection.

## SEO and Accessibility

- Add unique titles, descriptions, Open Graph text, canonical paths, semantic headings, useful alt text, and local-business structured data for every public content route.
- Optimize copy naturally for gym, fitness, training, spa, massage, and wellness searches around Shomolu and Lagos.
- Ensure keyboard navigation, visible focus states, labelled controls, good contrast, and reduced-motion support.

## Validation

- Verify every route and navigation target in the running site.
- Test desktop at 1280px and mobile at 390px for overflow, readable type, menu behavior, forms, tabs, dialogs, QR display, and consistent shared elements.
- Check browser console errors and run focused route/link validation before completion.
